About Me

CM

Currently · ALX Africa — Community & Operations

I am a Community Operations and Data Analytics professional passionate about building meaningful communities and using data to create better experiences for the people they serve.

My work sits at the intersection of community engagement, programme execution, operations, and analytics — from planning and coordinating events and engagement initiatives to tracking participation, gathering feedback, analysing trends, and translating insights into actionable improvements.

Through my experience at ALX Africa, I have supported learner and alumni engagement, volunteer coordination, hub operations, community events, reporting, and data-driven decision-making.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where people, programmes, and data come together to create stronger communities and measurable impact.
